# America Satellite — smooth still-scene export (V4)

This ZIP contains the complete 23:16 H.264 video at 1920×1080 and 30 fps.
The 100 non-map illustration scenes use a steady, gentle camera move to remove
the distracting jitter. Map scenes and the original audio are preserved.

Download `america-satellite-full-v4.zip` and extract it to get
`satellite-full-v4-smooth-1080p-30fps.mp4` (about 1.36 GB).

On Android, download the ZIP, open it in Files or ZArchiver, then choose
Extract. Keep enough free storage for the ZIP and the extracted MP4.
