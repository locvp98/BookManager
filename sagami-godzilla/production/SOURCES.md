# Sources and geographic construction

- Satellite: Esri World Imagery, cached z11 Web Mercator tiles. Map footer credits Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community. `public/maps/kanto-satellite.json` records bounds and tile origin. No map imagery from the older films was copied.
- Locator points use longitude/latitude: Tokyo (139.70,35.68), Sagami Bay (139.32,35.16), Tokyo Bay (139.83,35.43), Kanagawa (139.35,35.46), Miura Peninsula (139.65,35.21). These are representative positions, not administrative boundary centroids or surveyed boundary coordinates.
- Bay glows are location guides, not geographic boundary datasets. The Miura trace is a generalized locator line, not an exact coastline or hazard boundary. The westward arrow compares locations; it is not a route, current or monster trajectory.
- Satellite imagery includes real relief shading; this preview is a 2D satellite locator, not a 3D DEM scene. No claim about ocean depth is represented in the opening 40 seconds.
- All three new image assets are generated illustrations. Monster and cinema images are original fictional depictions, not licensed film footage, posters, historical evidence or verification of release dates/plot claims.
- Japanese narration/SRT supplied by user. Original audio is used from 0–40s, with a 0.2s volume tail to avoid abrupt preview cutoff. Full audio is retained separately. No burned-in narration subtitles.
